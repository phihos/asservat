# V2 typed owner proof, regional channel and hub coordinator

This seven-file component follows the integrated v2 source/producer. It is not runtime selection or a complete framed route.

The actual regional standby is constructed without a query/native proof. Its one-use observation method receives the concrete locator-owned OwnerObservationOperation. The owner wrapper accepts an already-opened native handle, calls Original once, captures its request/pin/participants and original context, and keeps the routing record as the sole Close owner. Its constructor transfers native ownership only on success; the constructing caller must close an untransferred native handle after failure. One stream may attach, sealing actual exporter, serving leaf and exchange. The selected coordinator borrows that state and never closes it after successful completion. The later concrete locator record must supply the exact retained pointer; request IDs alone cannot find/recreate proof. That pending-record/holder-sequence wiring is not implemented in this checkpoint.

The owner wrapper does genuine Final I/O once before ordinal four. Later result checks consume exactly those retained facts with their original dates, plus actual current SQL, both pinned/current issuer validation and exact process key/leaf. They never reopen Original or claim another independent native observation. General OwnerAuthenticator/source ports are unchanged. Source-policy protection and independently observed workload-guard protection remain distinct digests.

The regional channel owns one original stream root and one native producer operation. Every ordinal reads a new full source value; the two SQL49 invocations consume the same source instance. Current owner candidate/registration/root, actual TLS, local key/leaf and original native bounds continue at network/metadata boundaries. Final native mutation, source/role change, current SQL loss, expiry or unknown terminal outcome suppress result. Receipt and EOF completion are a mandatory closed stream port; an actual v2 framed implementation remains the immediate follow-up. No hidden v1 codec/cache or negotiation is introduced.

Tests use actual process-owned private keys and mutual TLS/h2 exporters; native and SQL observations and the terminal stream are synthetic component ports. Exact-integer codecs, wrong purpose/correlation, final mutation, pair substitution, cancellation/join and original lifetime are tested. This does not measure installed five-second availability or qualify the full HWORK→B→A→R path. Existing original rate/deadline/candidate bounds and v1 format/public behavior remain unchanged.
