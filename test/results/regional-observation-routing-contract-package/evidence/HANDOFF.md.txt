challenge, permission root and regional cluster, independently authenticated
holder installation/hub cluster/issuer/Pod/process, original exchange and exact
nonzero uint64 sequence. Permission root and installation are independent pins;

fields, malformed/noncanonical queries, wrong purposes, changed tuple/bounds

and `CanonicalizeRawInts(false)` to avoid RFC8785's default rounding beyond
2^53. Existing observation encoders, canonical digest owner and four proof

producer is added. No legacy behavior is replaced, so there is no applicable
preexisting public behavior to reproduce before these new value APIs compile.
The integer tests explicitly cover 2^53 boundaries and MaxUint64, rejection of
overflow/fraction/exponent/string/zero, and exact nested round trips. They do
not reinterpret old wire formats.
