# ADR-163: original-work transport observation lease

Status: accepted for implementation; component and native qualification pending.

ADR-135's private context exchange repeated the complete local process and peer
observation before every identity frame. The native bare-Pod process adapter
performs ten requests on one executor REST client and two on its independent
probe client for each uncontended process observation. Seven identity round
trips, the post-standby work check and final result/receipt require seventeen
transport observations. Even a full burst of twenty at the unchanged twenty
requests per second cannot complete those 170 executor requests in five seconds.
This excludes additional application process, target, SQL, key and local-start
work; independent REST clients must not be added into one rate calculation.

The preserved native journey 9 refused the fourth identity round trip at the
regional `receive-peer/unavailable` boundary, with zero collector calls and a
4.919341134-second whole public request. That identifies neither the exact
local source predicate nor a particular limiter refusal. A separate transport
regression actually reproduced seventeen observations and failure during the
sixth identity Send under the original five-second, twenty-QPS/twenty-burst
budget. Its ten-token observations model the enumerated executor graph; it is
not a native Kubernetes performance measurement.

Keep one transport observation lease inside the original frame stream. The hub
obtains its complete exact receiver when sending its one work frame. The region
obtains its complete exact receiver after the passive work frame arrives. The
setup/standby observation is never reused as this work lease. Retain the original
observation start, exact immutable channel (root, exchange, challenge, connector
leaf and both process identities) and earliest observation/caller deadline.
An already accepted work cannot obtain a replacement lease after expiry or
failure. There is no background renewal, persisted lease or global cache.

Only correctly ordered identity questions and answers reuse this observation.
Each question still reaches the original live human `CurrentIdentity`; groups,
scopes, permission, recovery, retention, process or effect checks are not cached
by this change. Direction and phase are checked before incoming frame content
reaches its semantic consumer. A second work frame, premature receipt, unsolicited
identity response, interleaved result or frame after the terminal receipt fails
closed. Existing frame, byte, connection and identity-check limits are unchanged.

The regional result Send and hub result Receive each require another complete
current peer observation, after the preceding blocking application/transport
work. These checks use the original lease deadline and exact channel. They may
shorten its end, never replace its start or extend any bound. The terminal receipt
uses that final, clipped interval. The final human identity check also remains.
A current negative cannot fall back to the earlier positive observation.

Expiry has a retained cancellation owner even between frames. Known stream/peer
failure, EOF, original caller/owner cancellation and failed final validation are
terminal; socket, reader, producer and deadline callbacks are joined before
release. A late successful producer cannot publish through cancellation. The
regional runner's independently current preparation, local CAS, capture and
post-storage checks remain unchanged. An application denial permits no further
effect; its existing finite unavailable disposition still needs the final full
peer check. Unknown effects remain spent, without automatic replay or reselection.

This selects bounded transport freshness: an otherwise unobserved remote source
or registration revocation may remain undiscovered during identity-frame traffic
until the original lease expires (at most five seconds, usually less). It does
not promise instantaneous revocation across processes. Known transport negatives
invalidate immediately, and actual effects still require their existing current
checks. A lease never authorizes an artifact, decryption, publication or download.

The regional transport graph falls from seventeen full observations to two for
this seven-round-trip path. This is a structural reduction, not a guaranteed
five-second end-to-end latency. Aged source intervals, the deployed connector
proof graph, SQL/key latency and the unmodified effect checks still need actual
native qualification. No wire format, role, schema, QPS, deadline, public route,
Helm enablement, activation mode or automatic continuation is added.
