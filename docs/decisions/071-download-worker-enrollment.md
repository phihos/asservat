# ADR-071 — Audited acquisition credentials and bounded renewal

Date: 2026-10-10. Status: implemented component refinement; scoped SQL/S3 and Kubernetes/OpenBao/TLS qualification passed. Runtime/bootstrap/local-CA composition remains pending.

ADR-068 checks a certificate-bound worker, but initial enrollment cannot require the certificate it is about to issue. Separate the current Pod/token/CSR-key proof from the later certificate-bound proof. Reuse the exact audited SQL dispatch, administrator profile and dedicated-audience TokenReview checks. Never mint a placeholder certificate or trust a caller-selected Pod label to break the dependency.

The worker generates its own private key and identity-free signed CSR. Trusted download control retains the human credential/current authorization; the worker receives neither that credential nor issuer/root keys. A new private enrollment coordinator verifies CSR possession, current admitted Pod/token and download authority before binding one immutable public key to the worker. Publish its encrypted enrollment audit through an independent role before spending any provider call.

Certificates use the existing client-only acquisition URI and strict X.509 profile. A certificate is usable only after exact signed-result persistence, encrypted certificate audit and separately credentialed activation. Verification of a CA signature alone cannot populate the worker registry or grant evidence access. Replays must return the same recorded certificate without calling the issuer or encrypting its acknowledged audit again. The later evidence unwrap still needs the original current download batch, privacy, capacity, recovery and worker proofs.

Long downloads need credential renewal. Derive twenty-minute certificate periods from the original worker start; the next period may be prepared one minute early. Cap every certificate at the period end and original worker deadline, rounding down to X.509 seconds. For an eight-hour worker there are at most 24 periods and two nonrefundable provider calls per period. Uncertain signing stays spent; only after its original finite signing interval may a fresh proof/current authority spend the second call. A new CSR signature, token, process restart, request ID or polling cycle cannot change the key or refill a period. Old and new activated certificates may coexist only within their original finite overlap, avoiding interruption of already admitted TLS work. A replacement Pod requires a separately admitted worker.

Migration 27 stores immutable public enrollment, attempts, certificates, audit references and activation. It contains no token, private key, native evidence or provider credential. Enrollment/signing, audit and activation use different SQL credentials; they cannot alter parent authority/counters or schedule Jobs. Current worker identity accepts only a matching activated certificate for that worker. Expired/profile-revoked workers cannot enroll or renew; cleanup remains independent.

The initial external adapter uses the existing maintained OpenBao client and standard Go X.509 verification, a fixed sign-only PKI role and no provider retries. The provider-neutral port also belongs in the accepted local-CA path; a test CA is not that implementation. Protected issuer installation/rotation, the bootstrap HTTPS/control transport, worker private-key lifetime, supervisor and Helm composition remain required. No public download endpoint is enabled by these components.

[Kubernetes documents bound-token TokenReview](https://kubernetes.io/docs/reference/access-authn-authz/service-accounts-admin/). [Vault's PKI API](https://developer.hashicorp.com/vault/api-docs/secret/pki) documents CSR signing, absolute expiration and role-controlled SAN/usages; actual OpenBao compatibility is an integration requirement, not inferred from the related API. Current tests and remaining limits belong in [implementation progress](../implementation-progress.md).

[ADR-072](072-worker-credential-channel.md) subsequently supplies the private enrollment transport and process-owned key/renewal client. Protected bootstrap, active request-owner routing and the acquisition executable remain pending.
