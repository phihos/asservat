## Notes

- 335 D16 consistency bugs found: retention max 3660 d (chart) vs 3650 d (CRD); `deletionBudgetSeconds` ≤10 y (chart)
  vs ≤1 d (CRD); hub-api pool × replicas can exceed the hard-coded PG `max_connections`; SeaweedFS 64-volume cap
  below the PVC size; `_services.tpl` `default 1` ignores `replicas: 0`.
- Template `fail` calls with numeric conditions: T/keycloak-deployment.yaml:20-21 (maxConnections ≠ 5),
  T/keycloak-deployment.yaml:23-24 (bundled PG port must be 5432 — KEEP, it is the bundled Service port),
  T/_helpers.tpl:251-252 (retainedSeconds formula), T/_hubapi.tpl:115 (connector profiles ≥ connectors,
  "fixed at first install"). The other ~37 `fail`s are presence/format checks, not numeric limits.
