## Top user-visible REMOVE items (cross-part)

1. Lifetime `max_captures` counter never decrements, so captures are refused permanently once it is reached. `internal/adapters/postgres/processing_capture.go:25,51`, migration 00050. Covered by 330 D7 and 335 D2.
2. Stored artifact size is capped at 256 MiB, so large cores and pcaps cannot be kept. `migrations/00095_streaming_custody_256mib.sql:11`. No ADR covers it.
3. Joined clusters are capped at 8, 15 or 16 depending on the layer. `internal/contracts/owner_cluster_catalog.go:26`, `internal/bootstrap/hub_api_remote_clusters.go:34`, `migrations/00065_regional_capacity.sql:68`, `charts/asservat/values.schema.json:400` (maxItems 15). `internal/application/configuration/reconciler.go:233` silently drops clusters beyond 8. Only partly covered (330 D2/D13).
4. Concurrent captures: 16 per installation (`internal/bootstrap/public_context_compose.go:195,347`), with a chart default of 1 per cluster (`charts/asservat/values.yaml:243`). Covered by 335 D8; the per-cluster default should be DERIVE to at least 8.
5. Active policies: 16 per cluster (`internal/domain/capture/policy_trigger.go:28`, `internal/application/capture/policy_activation.go:235`). Above 64, evaluation fails for all policies (`internal/adapters/postgres/policy_trigger.go:290`). Covered by 335 D8.
6. Shares last 24 h by default and 7 d at most (`internal/domain/authorization/share.go:20-21`, `internal/application/sharing/share.go:157`, `migrations/00086_evidence_share.sql:43`), with a 7-day policy-sharing end (`internal/application/capture/policy_activation.go:357`). Covered by 335 D7. The caps of 32 shares per artifact and 8 recipients or managers (`internal/adapters/postgres/evidence_share.go:322,364`, `migrations/00086_evidence_share.sql:29,31`) are not covered.
7. Approval and submit deadlines: a reviewer has 1 h (`internal/domain/authorization/grant.go:24`, `migrations/00097_access_grant.sql:77`); the requester has 5 min to submit after approval (`internal/transport/presentation/grant_operations.go:369`, `internal/adapters/postgres/context_reviewed.go:80,104`); previews expire after 2–5 min with 10 per user (`internal/adapters/postgres/context_preview.go:137,144`, `charts/asservat/values.yaml:244`). Covered by 335 D5/D9 and 330 D9.
8. Download caps: 1024 requests per session, 8 h per session, 2 downloads per user, 16 per installation, 1 h of worker runtime, and 2 downloads of a profile artifact. `internal/domain/artifact/download.go:110`, `internal/domain/artifact/profile_download_budget.go:6`, `internal/adapters/postgres/download.go:84,438`, `charts/asservat/values.yaml:246-261,362`. Covered by 335 D9, ADR-312 and 330 D4.
9. Permission scale: more than 1024 bindings, 4096 assignments, 256 assignments per workload, or 4096/1000 namespaces or workloads makes all authorization or observation unavailable. More than 32 audience assignments refuses capture, and permission sources beyond the 4th grant nothing. `migrations/queries/permissions.sql:51,72`, `internal/adapters/postgres/permissions.go:115`, `internal/domain/configuration/permissions.go:161`, `internal/domain/configuration/workloads.go:14`, `internal/domain/configuration/review.go:32,87`, `internal/domain/authorization/eligibility.go:286`. No ADR covers these.
10. Browser sessions: 10 per user, plus installation caps of 256 sessions, 64 logins and 256 logout events. When the logout table is full, an identity-provider logout is silently not applied. `internal/adapters/postgres/browser.go:119,175,182,338`, `charts/asservat/values.yaml:44-46`. Covered by 335 D8.

Next most visible:
- Live watches end after 30 s idle or 1 h, with 16 MiB of output (335 D9).
- Recorder windows are 15 s for packets and HTTP headers and 60 s for syscalls, and saved logs cover 60 s (335 D8).
- MCP tool calls get 5 s (330 D1/D5).
- Workload resolution fails above 256 Pods because the list has no continuation (`internal/adapters/kubernetes/workload_targets.go:22`).
- Configuration revisions are never pruned, so changes are refused after 1024.
- A Pod-status snapshot is capped at 4 KiB (DERIVE).
- Packet and syscall captures stop at 8 MiB, labelled "interim" in the code (DERIVE).
- hub-api runs one replica with `Recreate` (335 D13).

## Configurations that pass the schema and then break a component (335 D16)

- PostgreSQL `max_connections` is hard-coded at 117/122, but the schema accepts larger pool × replica products.
- Retention passes the chart schema up to 3660 d, but the CRD allows only 3650 d. `deletionBudgetSeconds` allows about 10 years in the chart but 1 day in the CRD.
- SeaweedFS `-volume.max=64` caps the bundled evidence store at 64 GiB.
- `replicas: 0` still deploys one replica because of `default 1`.

## Conflicts to settle

- ADR-311 keeps a 30 s authority reuse, while 330 D1 says caches do not expire by time. This decides `internal/adapters/postgres/capture.go:589`.
- ADR-222 keeps a pool of 32 physical connections, while 335 D11 caps an external PostgreSQL pool at 30 in total.
