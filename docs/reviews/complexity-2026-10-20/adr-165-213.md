**Original observation capability selection**

**Recommendation:** simplify. **Confidence:** medium.

**Qualification:** Component-3 passed; later installed composition selects v2. No general upgrade campaign shown.

| Complexity question | Assessment |
| --- | --- |

**Recommendation:** simplify. **Confidence:** high.

**Source:** [docs/decisions/177-hub-sql-connection-governor.md](../../decisions/177-hub-sql-connection-governor.md)

**Provenance:** Engineering accommodation of per-purpose NOINHERIT roles and32-connection cap; role-per-function is not documented user requirement.

| 5. Added privileges or exposure | Same process retains all purpose credentials; protects query mistakes, not process compromise. |
