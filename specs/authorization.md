# Authorization, approvals, sharing and audit

## REQ-AUT-001 — Identity and ordinary access

Human sessions support a qualified external OIDC provider or the planned [local identity alternative](deployment-profiles.md#req-dep-002--local-identity-without-a-second-permission-system). External sessions use standard OIDC code/PKCE and bounded session refresh; local identity must preserve equivalent principal, assurance and revocation semantics and standard OAuth access for MCP/CLI. Synchronize complete verified external groups by replacement, including removals; stored profile data and session cookies cannot renew claim freshness. Local membership is read from authoritative local identity state with the same bounded freshness/effect checks. There is no mandatory directory-admin account or personal offline token for recurring team capture. IdP logout and external deprovisioning are distinct; login-only synchronization cannot guarantee immediate external revocation. Qualified back-channel logout may invalidate sessions; SCIM/security-event integrations remain optional. The accepted recurring execution model is [team policy automation](policy-automation.md).

A native browser download holds no server-side refresh ownership ([ADR-224](../docs/decisions/224-remove-download-refresh-owners.md)). Read-only GET requests never refresh a session; an interrupted download resumes after the console's explicit refresh.

Every user-facing operation MUST resolve a principal `(issuer, subject, clientID, identityType)` from validated credentials. Validate signature, issuer, audience, expiry/not-before and configured client binding. Never accept ID tokens as access tokens or trust client-supplied group membership or roles. Normalize `azp`/`client_id` with an issuer-specific mapping; reject ambiguous claims.

The installation serves one organization. Administrators configure its trusted issuers, audiences, clients and eligible organization users/groups. A valid token from an allowed issuer alone does not establish organization eligibility; the configured directory or membership policy MUST admit the identity. This eligibility does not require ordinary workload access. No tenant or organization ID is supplied by clients or stored on principals, grants, shares or artifacts.

The [initial adapter profile](../docs/architecture/identity-eligibility.md) implements bounded RFC 9068/RS256 access-token checks with one resource audience, explicit complete group assertions and current protected organization/client policy. This internal implementation does not claim arbitrary OIDC provider compatibility or change the product's provider-adapter boundary. The [Keycloak profile](../docs/architecture/keycloak-profile.md) now has bounded real-provider qualification for access-token, group-refresh and separate client-class behavior. Operator realm configuration, public browser/MCP sessions, step-up and complete accepted policy composition still require qualification before public use. Tokens and arbitrary backend errors MUST NOT enter ordinary logs or error responses.

Ordinary access is namespace/workload entitlement across enrolled clusters, granted through administrator-managed identity-group mappings. Entitlements include the cluster UUID, so identical namespace/workload names in different clusters do not confer access. Kubernetes RBAC controls resource submission. An administrator may explicitly enable the trusted metadata or exact access-review mappings in [permission management](permissions.md); RBAC is not inferred as an evidence ACL without that configured source. Query identity adapters are distinct from Kubernetes service-account identities. Unauthenticated errors reveal no artifact existence.

### SC-AUT-001

Given matching subject strings from two issuers or a token for another audience, when a case is queried, then the identities are not conflated and the wrong-audience token is rejected before case discovery.

## REQ-AUT-002 — Authorization composition

A read MUST satisfy all applicable conditions:

```text
valid, organization-eligible identity AND artifact retained AND no deny
AND (ordinary evidence entitlement OR active EvidenceShare coverage)
AND required OAuth scope
AND (if raw: active per-principal/client raw operation grant)
AND remaining query/output budget AND durable audit availability
```

Ordinary evidence entitlement is evaluated using the role and retained-audience rules in [permission management](permissions.md); a basic evidence-reader role does not alone confer raw eligibility. A scope alone is never a grant. A share alone is never a raw grant or capture permission. Deny policy takes precedence over both. A group share must be evaluated against current qualified group membership; raw grants remain bound to the actual calling principal/client even if the share targets a group.

Perform current ordinary target/scope checks before an evidence lookup used by that path. Recheck identity/source/evidence deadlines after blocking reads; time spent resolving another dependency cannot extend freshness. The first ordinary evaluator covers only context eligibility and approved evidence read/download eligibility. Its success cannot substitute for accepted execution, local ceilings, grant/share conditions, durable audit or the final effect gate. Its matching assignments describe the caller, not every team that belongs in a capture's default audience.

### SC-AUT-002

Given a user with `evidence:raw` and a valid raw grant but no ordinary entitlement or share, when memory is requested, then access is denied. Adding an extract-only share still does not permit raw access.

## REQ-AUT-003 — Individual and policy grants

`AccessGrant` distinguishes artifact queries, automatic policies and individual captures. Raw grants bind existing artifact digests and operations. Policy grants bind policy UID, collection execution digest, workload/recipe scope and finite future budgets. Ad hoc grants bind one accepted capture UID and its execution digest, without a fabricated policy digest. [Execution identity](execution.md) defines digest separation; unknown future artifacts are never raw-query grant targets.

Individual grants require a distinct authenticated human approver with the configured approver role for the target workload/evidence scope and recent step-up authentication. An approval cannot expand the requester's underlying collection or evidence entitlement. Default expiry: 1 hour for ad hoc capture; 8 hours maximum for a raw-query grant. Policy grants default to seven days. Protected installation approval settings configure a finite default and maximum (initial configurable range up to 365 days; example maximum 30 days), with narrower local maxima winning. Longer periods require explicit reviewed expiry and do not renew themselves. Pin approval settings and issuedAt; reject expiry beyond the effective maximum. Short execution permits, current authorization, maximum counts/bytes, image/target restrictions and invalidation on collection-scope changes are unchanged. Lowering the maximum clamps existing grants from their original issuedAt; raising it never extends existing grants. Sharing and raw-query maxima stay separate. Grant ownership cannot be transferred.

For a recurring policy, the grant retains the authenticated human/service requester as provenance and independently binds `policyExecutor` plus `policyAuthority`. Self-approval compares the human reviewer with the authenticated human requester, never merely with the machine executor. The recurring grant does not confer personal resultAccess, raw-query, download or sharing rights; creator departure alone does not invalidate independent team authority.

The approval page MUST show exact scope and impact, support approve/deny/revoke, protect against CSRF/clickjacking, and record issuer/subject/client of requester and approver, reason, authentication context and policy digest. Automation/workload identities cannot approve themselves. MFA/step-up is verified through qualified external IdP assurance or server-verified local authentication under REQ-DEP-002, never a checkbox in the UI. Local accounts do not relax independent-human review.

### SC-AUT-003

Given a raw or capture request, when its requester attempts approval through another browser/client under the same human identity, then self-approval is refused. A reviewed policy can run automatic approved actions until its expiry/budget; a changed recipe requires review again.

## REQ-AUT-004 — Budgets, reservations and revocation

Use an authoritative hub SQL transaction to reserve cumulative grant bytes/executions with central capacity and dispatch work before execution. Local prepaid permits use bounded Kubernetes compare-and-swap; see [persistence](persistence.md). A reservation has a unique operation ID, bounds, expiry, state and one consuming cluster/node identity. Retries reuse the same reservation, never allocate an extra execution accidentally. Bound active reservation rows per grant; completed outcomes move to immutable audit while authoritative aggregate spent counters remain in SQL.

`maxExecutions` counts individual authorized action starts for capture grants and individual query operations for raw-query grants. A recipe with three intrusive actions and one process per action consumes three executions, not one; internal process fanout adds one execution per additional actual start. Reserve each action's worst-case byte bound before starting it; the UI shows both action count and maximum bundle cost. Nonintrusive actions still count toward the ordinary policy/node budgets. These permission-grant counters are independent of [capacity reservations](capacity.md), which cover workload-wide starts, physical-impact limits, persistent storage and fair transfer service. Both must succeed before execution.

Default active-reservation ceiling is 64 per grant. Query concurrency defaults to 4 per principal and 32 per installation. Reject or queue within a bounded deadline when those limits are reached; never grow active grant state indefinitely.

Permission-source freshness also bounds permit expiry as specified in [permission management](permissions.md); no cache or permit renewal may extend a stale authorization decision. The hub may delegate a prepaid slice of a policy budget to a local cluster permit; local reservations consume only that slice. Default permit expiry is <=60 seconds. On ambiguous execution, charge the full reservation conservatively and report `Indeterminate`. Reclaim unused budget only after authoritative proof that execution never started; expiry by itself is not that proof. A grant that reaches its active-reservation ceiling queues or rejects work.

Revocation MUST invalidate new reservations, cursors, sessions and shares-dependent queries. Connected workers receive cancellation; disconnected intrusive work stops accepting starts at permit expiry. State the residual <=permit lifetime revocation window. Started uploads may finish with no new acquisition only while artifact privacy/retention deadlines remain valid. Each query rechecks authorization before delivering output, not merely when the sandbox started.

### SC-AUT-004

Given two simultaneous raw requests sharing a 64 KiB remaining budget, when each asks for 64 KiB, then at most one reservation succeeds. A controller crash after dispatch cannot make the same intrusive action run twice. Revocation during analysis prevents result delivery.

## REQ-SHR-001 — Explicit evidence sharing within the organization

An evidence owner with `evidence:share` and an administrator-enabled sharing policy MAY create an `EvidenceShare` that grants named users or stable identity-provider group IDs access despite their ordinary evidence ACL. Concrete share creation requires current access to each included artifact and owner/delegated sharing authority; merely receiving a share never confers sharing authority.

A share MUST bind case, an explicit snapshot of artifact digests or extract IDs, recipient user/group IDs, allowed data classes/operations, reason, immutable creating actor, accepted management assignment and expiry. Default expiry is 24 hours, bounded by remaining artifact retention and a 7-day installation-wide maximum. Future recurrences, future artifacts, future extracts and changed transformations are excluded by default; extending the snapshot is a new auditable decision. An explicit collection-time SharingIntent under REQ-SHR-004 through REQ-SHR-007 can authorize later concrete snapshot revisions for its pinned outputs, without turning a standalone share into an open-ended future subscription. No anonymous token/link capability. URLs are navigation links requiring authentication.

Recipients MUST be eligible organization users/groups under the installation's configured identity policy, checked at creation and again at use. An unapproved issuer, an identity outside that organization policy, or evidence from another installation MUST be rejected. A group identifier must be stable, issuer-scoped and recognized by the configured directory; a display name is insufficient. Identical group names or IDs under another issuer do not inherit access.

### SC-SHR-001

Given a developer without the payments workload ACL, when an authorized owner shares two existing stack extracts with that user's organization group, then the user can query those extracts. A later occurrence and an unshared file remain inaccessible. A recipient from an unapproved issuer or outside the organization identity policy is rejected even if the share creator is an administrator; share creation cannot alter that policy.

## REQ-SHR-002 — Shared view and raw access

Discovery MUST project a minimal shared case view: the permitted case ID/title metadata, authorized timeline entries, artifacts and available operations only. Do not expose hidden artifact names/counts, other cases, workload inventory, labels or search facets through counts, pagination or error differences. Cursors bind principal, authorization revision, share IDs and query snapshot.

Sharing raw evidence requires explicit raw-sharing authority and independent approver review; recipients still obtain their own operation grant. An extract share can be owner-approved under the configured sharing policy because it does not grant raw access. The sharing policy MAY require approval for every share. The approval page shows both the share and the raw grant when composing access.

### SC-SHR-002

Given a shared case with one visible extract and three hidden artifacts, when the recipient lists, searches, paginates or guesses IDs, then no hidden count/name/existence is disclosed. An approved raw share without an individual raw-query grant returns `GrantRequired`.

## REQ-SHR-003 — Revocation and group freshness

Currently authorized personal/team managers and authorized administrators MUST be able to revoke immediately (REQ-SHR-008). Artifact expiry, removal from organization identity eligibility, explicit deny and share expiry revoke access regardless of cached search results. Group membership MUST be revalidated by an approved IdP adapter or short-lived fresh assertions; default maximum membership staleness is 5 minutes. A token with stale group claims cannot extend that bound. Providers without usable group-freshness semantics support individual-user shares only until qualified.

Do not promise clawback of evidence already seen by a recipient or external AI agent. Audit creation, approval, recipient access, scope changes, expiry and revocation. No share operation writes a chat or email notification; users can copy a navigation URL or integrate content-free events themselves.

### SC-SHR-003

Given a recipient with a valid cursor, when the share is revoked, then the next page is denied even if the index cache still contains the case. Removal from a shared group stops new disclosure within the documented freshness bound.

### Implementation refinement (ADR-232)

The first implemented increment ([ADR-232](../docs/decisions/232-concrete-evidence-share.md)) narrows REQ-SHR-001 to REQ-SHR-003 without changing the canonical contracts. A share covers exactly one published pod-status artifact at its verified version, data class `metadata` and the `get_case` read (approved inspection of the same artifact honors it too), up to eight User or Group recipients with stable IDs of the configured issuer, and an expiry of at most seven days (a constant) that never outlives the artifact's effective access deadline. There is no administrator sharing-policy toggle: holding `evidence-owner` on the workload and the `evidence:share` client scope is the sharing authority. Management is the creator's contributing `evidence-owner` assignments, pinned by assignment ID and basis digest; a share is Suspended while none of them is current, and a re-granted binding is a new assignment, so it never restores an old share. Since increment 4 the pinned assignments are the creator's owner teams and every current team member manages the share (see the [team management refinement](#implementation-refinement-adr-232-increment-4)); administrators revoke through an explicit Deny AccessBinding or by removing `evidence-owner`. Recipient eligibility under the organization identity policy is enforced at every use (identity admission); there is no creation-time directory check. State is derived at read time (Active, Expired, Revoked, Suspended). The share is evaluated only after the ordinary evaluator refuses and before any evidence lookup, current denies win, and every read re-reads the share. Since [ADR-233](../docs/decisions/233-recipient-native-download.md) a share also grants the recipient the native download (`prepare_artifact_download`, `get_artifact_download` and the content route) of exactly the shared artifact version, with the `evidence:download` scope, charged to the workload's existing transfer allocation; within one content response the share is re-read at the existing 30-second reconstruction interval (ADR-220), so revocation, expiry or suspension refuses at the next authorization point. Raw access, sharing intents and cursors are later increments.

### SC-SHR-009

Given a published pod-status artifact and an eligible organization user named by no AccessBinding, when the user calls `get_case`, then it is refused exactly like an absent case. When an `evidence-owner` of the workload with `evidence:share` shares that artifact with the user, then the user's `get_case` returns only that artifact with access `shared`, and the user can prepare and Range-download its native file. After the owner revokes the share, the user's next `get_case`, `prepare_artifact_download` and content request are refused. A current Deny of the user, share expiry, or the removal of the owner's pinned assignment refuses the read as well; re-granting the owner role does not restore the share.

## REQ-SHR-004 — Share results when requesting collection

An evidence owner MAY include `spec.sharing` in a DebugCapture, in preset, composition or expanded form. It names eligible organization users/groups, selects outputs and optionally shortens expiry. It applies only to that accepted capture UID and immutable resolved plan, including its selected outputs produced by later analysis. It does not authorize another capture, another case occurrence or arbitrary future analysis. Omission means no additional sharing; ordinary evidence audiences remain unchanged. Collection still requires the submitter's collection rights, action approvals and capacity independently of sharing authority.

Default output selection is `approvedExtracts`: resolve the selected recipe's administrator-approved, versioned metadata/extract projections at acceptance, display them in preview, and pin their action IDs, projection digests, output classes and permitted read operations. No raw outputs are included by default. Omitted operations resolve to the selected projection’s permitted operations; explicit operations must be a subset. Sharing reason defaults to the capture reason or configured policy purpose, with an optional override, so the basic path asks only for recipients. Advanced `explicit` selection names action IDs and classes; metadata/extract selections require an immutable projection name/version, raw selections require explicit raw operations. Missing projections, unknown actions or excessive selection scope fail validation; do not infer a weaker projection or silently select every future output. At most 16 explicit selections and 32 resolved selections are permitted. The protected projection catalog binds each immutable name/version to allowed action kinds, analyzer/configuration digest, validated output class and permitted read operations. It is part of the shipped/admin-reviewed analysis catalog, with no arbitrary user transform or new configuration API. If a recipe has no approved projections, reject approvedExtracts with a remedy before collection. A projection release upgrade cannot broaden an existing instruction. The controller computes hashes and IDs; users do not enter them.

The authenticated submitter is the immutable `createdBy` audit actor. Resolve personal or team management under REQ-SHR-008; clients cannot submit a different creator or assignment proof. For direct Kubernetes/GitOps submissions, use the protected actor provenance under REQ-PER-007. Validate the submitter's owner/delegated sharing authority before accepting, and the pinned manager authority plus concrete artifact access at publication. A recurring automation identity needs its own sharing authority at acceptance; the controller's privileges never substitute for it.

Create a service-owned SQL SharingIntent with a UUID `id`, immutable source cluster/namespace/capture or policy UID/generation, submitted sharing digest, resolved plan/recipe digests, creator, management, recipients, resolved selections, expiry and limits. The accepted scope is immutable; authorizers/reconcilers own status/revision. It is a runtime record, not a CRD that maintainers create. The browser's optional “Share results with…” section and authenticated capture service/MCP request_capture use the same evaluator. MCP additionally needs evidence:share when sharing is present and remains disabled by default.



Default intent expiry is 24 hours from acceptance, with an installation maximum of 7 days that administrators may tighten; an explicit notAfter must be future and within that bound. Materialized share expiry is the minimum of intent expiry, applicable sharing-policy maximum and every included artifact's current read cutoff. Expiry never starts anew on upload/analysis completion. Return capture and sharing status separately. Valid collection may proceed while a sharing approval is pending or a sharing dependency becomes unavailable; it must not claim recipients already have access. An invalid/unauthorized initial sharing declaration rejects the combined request before execution rather than silently discarding requested sharing.

### SC-SHR-004

Given an owner requests context capture with an incident-response group and no explicit output selection, when collection and delayed approved log projection finish, then only the pinned metadata/extract outputs become shared automatically. The group cannot see raw logs, another capture or outputs of a subsequently installed projection version. A caller with collection permission but no sharing authority cannot attach this instruction.

## REQ-SHR-005 — Explicit sharing of recurring policy results

A DebugPolicy MAY include the same sharing declaration only with `futureCaptures.enabled: true`, an explicit `maxCaptures` (1–100) and absolute `notAfter`. Preview/review must explicitly say that repeated collections will be shared and show the maximum scope. There is no wildcard “all future workload evidence” option. Pin source policy UID/generation, complete normalized plan/recipe digests, executor, immutable creator and managing owner assignments at activation. The owner's review authorizes extract sharing where the installation permits owner approval; independent review is required wherever configured and always for raw selections. A capture-action approval does not constitute a sharing approval.

For automatic sharing, register each subsequently accepted policy capture against that exact intent before dispatch; registration is idempotent and atomically consumes one capture slot. Failed/empty/partial or ambiguous accepted captures conservatively consume their slot; duplicate delivery of the same capture UID does not. Registration may occur while one or all selections await approval: PendingApproval does not require delaying an otherwise authorized capture. Captures predating intent acceptance or lacking a durable registration are excluded; default audience access to them is unchanged. Hub unavailability or sharing-capacity exhaustion must not stop independently authorized policy collection, but the unregistered capture is reported SharingUnavailable and is never silently backfilled. Registering a capture grants no new collection right and never overrides storm/resource limits. Each capture gets its own concrete artifact snapshot; a shared case view never includes other occurrences merely because they correlate to the same case.

A policy spec/recipe/target/executor/recipient/selection change invalidates registration for the old revision and requires a new resolved sharing decision, plus any applicable independent approval. Metadata-only edits outside the normalized spec do not. A new revision does not backfill previous captures, reset an old intent's counters or extend its expiry. PendingApproval means no selection is approved; Active permits approved selections while other selections may remain pending. Draining forbids new registrations and Completed means registered publication work has settled; both preserve still-valid concrete shares. Suspended denies publication and dependent reads; Denied, Revoked and Expired cannot be renewed in place. At the capture limit, enter Draining: stop new registrations while previously registered captures may publish their pinned outputs before expiry. Registration and share quotas are separate from policy execution quotas, and all apply.

### SC-SHR-005

Given a reviewed policy instruction limited to ten captures and expiring at 18:00, when capture ten is registered and its extract finishes at 17:50, then it can be shared within remaining limits. Capture eleven, an old occurrence and a capture from a changed recipe are excluded. An extract ready after 18:00 is not shared, and restarting the controller does not renew the interval or counters.

## REQ-SHR-006 — Concrete publication and approval boundaries

Before materializing any share, validate current personal/team manager authority and retained artifact access, explicit denies, organization eligibility, group mapping/freshness, intent state/expiry, matching capture provenance, source plan, output selection, projection digest/classification, artifact commit/integrity and privacy restrictions/deadlines. Verify the complete derivation chain: every parent must originate in the registered capture and the selected action. Bound traversal to 256 distinct manifests and depth 64; unavailable parents, cycles or exceeded bounds deny automatic publication with an actionable reason. Ordinary analysis remains governed by its own limits. Mixed-capture comparisons, recipient query results, newly requested analyses and changed projections do not qualify automatically. Artifact labels or client-supplied case IDs cannot prove this provenance. No eligible output means an explicit NoEligibleOutputs result, not a share of the raw fallback.

Materialize ordinary EvidenceShare SQL records with concrete artifact IDs/digests, immutable origin references to intent/capture/selection, creator, management, operations and expiry. `includeFutureArtifacts` and `allowReshare` remain false. Add each newly ready artifact through an audited, compare-and-swap snapshot revision authorized by the pinned intent; the revision itself always enumerates its finite existing artifacts. A revision bump invalidates old cursors. Idempotency binds immutable intent ID, capture UID, selection ID and artifact digest; retries cannot duplicate a grant or extend its lifetime. Never union permissions across different selections in a way that grants a raw operation to another artifact. Use a separate bounded child share per capture/selection, and only add matching artifacts to it.

Approval is tracked per resolved output selection. Owner-approved metadata/extract selections may publish while raw selections await independent human approval. Raw approval binds the canonical digest of the immutable intent spec, selections, recipient set, source plan, capture/output limits and absolute expiry; one reviewed recurring decision may cover those future outputs within its limits. Materialized raw shares reference that recorded approval and preserve approver provenance; the controller is not their human approver. Approval must still be valid at publication and its revocation invalidates dependent shares. Recipients still require their own artifact/operation-bound raw-query grants; no grant for unknown future artifact IDs is manufactured. Sensitive payloads remain subject to all ordinary privacy, client and query-budget controls.

Bound distinct artifacts across all selections to 64 per capture by default (request may narrow). The maximum number of active child shares is resolved selection count × registered captures, additionally bounded by installation share/runtime quotas. Atomically account publication slots and artifact sets; duplicate events/selection overlap cannot inflate or reset counters. Apply backpressure with SharingCapacityUnavailable before granting access; the capture can remain useful to its normal audience. Store bounded counters and registrations in PostgreSQL; archive immutable outcomes in encrypted object storage. Registration atomically consumes the parent capture slot and binds the capture UID. Publication atomically reserves artifact/share slots and updates the concrete child snapshot, counters and audit outbox; pending audit scope is not readable. Enforce the unique keys and lock order in REQ-DB-002/003. Every dependent read requires current parent/selection authorization; SQL/S3/local writes are not one distributed transaction. Bound each intent to 100 registrations, each capture to 64 distinct artifacts and 32 children, with installation quotas as an additional ceiling. No per-recipient evidence copy is introduced, and shared references never extend retention.

### SC-SHR-006

Given a capture has an approved extract selection and a pending raw-core selection, when artifacts commit, then extracts become visible and the core remains unshared until independent raw-sharing approval. A projection containing unexpected raw fields, a foreign parent, forged capture provenance or a publication event after revocation is rejected. Replaying a valid event produces one snapshot entry and spends one artifact slot. Approved raw sharing alone still cannot execute read_memory.

## REQ-SHR-007 — Stop, revoke and explain automatic sharing

Any current manager under REQ-SHR-008 or authorized administrator can Stop future sharing or Revoke this instruction and its shares. Stop enters Draining: no new capture registrations, but already registered captures can finish publishing before expiry. Existing child shares remain until their own expiry/revocation. For an accepted single capture, cancellation of collection does not retract evidence already shared or its registered eligible partial results; the UI offers explicit revocation to prevent further disclosure. Completing collection does not stop delayed approved extracts.

Revocation changes the authoritative intent revision first, denies further materialization and all intent-dependent share reads, then reconciles child status. Read authorization checks current parent intent and approval revocations, not just child status; unavailable authoritative state fails closed under existing freshness rules; stale workers, cursors and cached indexes cannot bypass the parent denial. Independently granted ordinary access or unrelated shares remain independent. Revoking a particular child share permanently prevents automatic recreation/expansion for that intent/capture/selection. Persist that tombstone through the intent lifetime and any outstanding publication work. Revocation is terminal and a GitOps reapply of the same source generation cannot resurrect it; renewing sharing requires an explicit new accepted decision/generation.

Loss of all valid pinned management authority suspends publication and dependent reads under REQ-SHR-008 freshness rules; an unavailable unrelated manager source does not defeat a known valid manager when deny state is current. Resumption requires an explicit authorized decision and cannot silently restore an old ownership assignment. Intent expiry denies dependent access even if child cleanup is delayed. Policy suspension, deletion, spec revision or removal of the sharing declaration stops new registrations without automatically revoking previously shared captures; explain this in the edit/delete preview and provide an explicit revoke-all action. Capture/source deletion must not cascade-delete revocation records needed for retained evidence. Draining/Completed intents remain authoritative for already issued shares; GC only after dependent grants, evidence deadlines and replay windows no longer require them.

The UI shows recipients, exact selected outputs, activation/expiry, registered/remaining captures, outputs shared/pending/blocked, approval state and whether Stop or Revoke was chosen. Recipients discover a case only after at least one concrete artifact is shared; no empty pending case or other-team inventory leaks. The creator can copy the eventual authenticated case link; no outbound notifications or automatic exports are added. Explicit authorized artifact downloads use [the download contract](artifact-downloads.md). request_capture returns a sharing-intent ID/navigation URI when requested; list_shares can show owned intents and child origins, while received views remain concrete-artifact-only. revoke_share can target one share or the whole intent with an expected revision/idempotency key; it never changes capture authority or approves raw access.

### SC-SHR-007

Given an owner revokes an instruction while a worker publishes a late extract, when the worker retries or a recipient follows an old cursor, then no dependent disclosure occurs. Revoking just that child cannot cause its recreation on replay. Stopping a recurring instruction lets already registered captures finish within expiry while blocking new registrations; revoking it instead denies every dependent share immediately subject to the existing delivery checks.

## REQ-AUT-005 — Durable audit

Every **admitted authenticated** `tools/call`, UI/API evidence access, grant/share mutation, intrusive action start and authorization denial MUST produce an append-only audit intent and terminal outcome. Authenticate and enforce finite admission before application dispatch. Invalid credentials and pre-admission overload do not promise individually retained records for an unbounded rejected stream: retain bounded security/exhaustion evidence and saturating counters instead, with no evidence disclosure or effects. This finite admission boundary does not exempt a scope denial, invalid tool input or unknown tool after authenticated operation admission. Record principal identity, normalized operation, keyed argument digest, case/artifact/share/grant IDs, decision/reason, reservation ID, output size/digest and trace ID. Do not log raw arguments, tokens, matched log contents, memory or packet fields. Use a keyed digest where predictable secret arguments could be dictionary-attacked.

Commit intent before starting sensitive work and commit the result digest before sending evidence. If the client disconnects after result commit, record delivery unknown; never assert the client received it. If audit is unavailable, deny evidence disclosure and intrusive starts. Background capture may preserve already observed encrypted data while recording an audit gap, but cannot manufacture an approval. Sealing/checkpoints and qualified Object Lock within the separate finite audit-retention policy strengthen integrity; ordinary object storage alone is not immutable against an administrator.

### SC-AUT-005

Given a completed query result, when audit storage fails before result commit, then no result is sent. Retrying a mutation preserves its original business request key and exact accepted scope/reservation; each new public invocation receives a new server-owned audit request ID. Audit receipt reconciliation can recover the exact immutable audit record but cannot redispatch application work. No extra user-managed idempotency key is required for ordinary reads.

Audit admission reserves finite retained records, objects and bytes independently of workload evidence/control capacity, including a bounded number of lazily created principal accounts. Principal limits do not reset when the client or input/output MAC key changes. Protect finite resolution/cancellation audit and provider capacity from normal polling or denial traffic; current rights remain mandatory. Never refund uncertain writes on expiry, cancellation or object absence. Restore budgets only after qualified writer quiescence, object purge and independently acknowledged deletion evidence. Runtime enablement requires that lifecycle and bounded security/exhaustion handling; a disabled component with unreclaimed fixed charges is not a complete durable-audit implementation.

[ADR-112](../docs/decisions/112-healthy-operation-audit-retirement.md) defines the initial positive-writer retirement protocol: purge both acknowledged originals, use one freed object slot for the finite signed/encrypted deletion certificate, then independently verify terminal absence before removing identifying SQL state. No recursive fourth audit object is required for that preauthorized terminal cleanup. Retain the original record charge while qualified residual SQL history remains; bounded principal-HMAC cooling is still privacy-relevant. Freeze all deadlines and backup/WAL backing at admission. Late cleanup never extends authorized retention, and historical/unknown writes cannot acquire positive completion from a later GET.

[ADR-115](../docs/decisions/115-overdue-operation-audit-erasure.md) permits a bounded terminal exception only for newly admitted records with its immutable reviewed capability: after the fixed certificate proof window can no longer be met, both original positive writer closures and a durable no-certificate fence allow original-object purge without writing a late certificate. Independent current absence acknowledgement still precedes SQL retirement and capacity release. Record the missed proof window in a bounded non-identifying aggregate; never claim it or the original deletion deadline was met. Original encrypted intent/outcome audit remains mandatory. Existing records, uncertain writers, already claimed certificates, exhausted cleanup budgets and restart/restore require separate reconciliation; this exception cannot silently upgrade them.

## REQ-SHR-008 — Multiple owner teams and immutable creators

Several issuer-qualified IdP groups may hold evidence-owner for the same workload through AccessBindings or trusted metadata mappings. Owner is a sharing/reading role, not collection permission; workload-maintainer is granted separately. A share or instruction records immutable `createdBy` from authenticated submission and `management` resolved either to that creator or 1–8 stable owner groups with pinned assignment IDs. No group member list is copied. The creator is never replaced by a controller, a group or the last editor.

Authoring may request `management: {mode: creator}` or `mode: teams` with eligible group references. When omitted, default to the submitter's currently valid owner-team assignments covering the whole scope; preview the exact teams before acceptance. If none exist, use the creator only if individually authorized. Do not silently add a co-owner group the submitter does not belong to. Selecting additional existing owner teams requires scoped delegation to assign management (normally evidence administrator); show that requirement in preview. More than eight or incompatible per-artifact assignments require explicit narrowing, not silent truncation. SAR-derived maintainership alone supplies neither team ownership nor sharing authority.

Any current member of a pinned managing group can inspect authorized management metadata, stop future sharing or revoke an instruction/child, provided their evidence-owner assignment is still valid and no deny applies. A member does not need a fresh approval just to revoke. Publication/read authorization depends on at least one still-valid pinned management assignment and the original acceptance/approval, not continued employment of the creating person. For a personal instruction the creator's eligibility/authority must remain current. Revalidate organization eligibility, current group/source state and retained scope under ordinary freshness limits; unknown authoritative deny state fails closed. Loss of every manager suspends publication/dependent reads and requires explicit authorized resume. Group membership changes within the same valid assignment preserve team management, including employee departure.

Management authority never supplies missing artifact-read or raw-query rights. A management-only view exposes opaque IDs, expiry and revoke/stop controls, not hidden evidence, unrelated recipients or other teams' membership. Initial team assignments must cover the accepted target/output scope; concrete publication additionally requires their retained audience/access eligibility. Merely assigning a team to management, receiving a share or acquiring future workload ownership does not widen the historical artifact audience or confer reshare rights.

Changing the pinned manager set is an explicit audited replacement/transfer decision, not a group-membership refresh or an edit to immutable scope. Preview separately “Manage existing sharing” and “Read existing evidence”; the latter needs a concrete share/authorized historical ownership decision. Replacement carries remaining counts, original deadlines, registration origins and revocation tombstones; it cannot backfill excluded captures or reset budgets. Widened manager/scope transfers need fresh applicable approvals, always independent review for raw, before activation. For recurring policies, use a newly accepted generation for future captures and leave old instructions draining or explicitly revoked. Authority recovery can be performed by a scoped administrator without changing the recorded creator.

Raw-sharing review always names a distinct authenticated human from the requesting/creating actor, even when both belong to the same managing team or use different clients. A qualified action-approver from a managing team may approve another person's request; group co-ownership is not itself approval and does not require a vote from every team. Extraction approvals by another valid manager record that actual actor. Approval binds the accepted management/scope digest. AccessGrant requester/client bindings remain nontransferable.

UI and MCP `list_shares(view=owned)` include currently manageable personal/team instructions, not everything the caller can read. Show “Created by” and “Managed by” separately to authorized managers; recipients keep a minimal concrete-artifact view. The default capture workflow still only asks for recipients; selecting manager teams is optional advanced control. [Two-team binding](../examples/access-binding-owners.yaml), [resolved team intent](../examples/sharing-intent.yaml).

### SC-SHR-008

Given two accepted managing owner teams, when the creator leaves and another current authorized member revokes the share, then revocation succeeds and provenance still identifies the creator. Losing one team does not disable a valid remaining team; losing all suspends dependent access. A newly added namespace owner, a recipient, a forged group reference or the same creator in another browser cannot take historical ownership or self-approve raw sharing. Transfer does not reset counts or expiry.

### Implementation refinement (ADR-232 increment 4)

[ADR-232](../docs/decisions/232-concrete-evidence-share.md#increment-4-team-management) implements default management for concrete shares only; there is no `management` selection, transfer or replacement. Management is the creator's contributing `evidence-owner` Group assignments covering the artifact (its retained audience), at most eight; more are refused with `ManagementTooBroad` instead of being truncated. Only a creator without any owner team pins the individual User assignment (creator-only management). A current manager is a human with `evidence:share` who holds one pinned assignment that is still exactly a current allow of the target and is not named by a current deny; group membership changes within the same assignment keep management, so the share survives the creator's departure. A manager can list (`list_shares(view=owned)` returns created shares in every phase plus unrevoked, unexpired managed shares, each with `revision`, `created_by` and `managed_by`; received rows stay minimal) and revoke. The creator can always revoke their own share, since revocation only restricts access. A newly added owner binding, even for the same group, is a new assignment ID and manages no older share. Management grants no read of the artifact: reading still needs the ordinary rights or a share.

## REQ-AUT-006 — Sensitive observation sessions

Raw packet rings, HTTP parsing and payload-sensitive predicates need authority before any sensitive observation, including discarded bytes. A frozen-output maxBytes is insufficient. Grants for these capabilities MUST additionally specify observationLimits: finite maxSessionSeconds (initial maximum one hour), cumulative maxObservedBytes across the grant, maxBytesPerSecond and maxConcurrentSessions. Charge wire bytes entering the sensitive parser/predicate, including retransmissions, before processing; reserve conservative bounded byte slices before dispatch. Lease renewal/session restart never refills the grant. Stop at exhaustion with `ObservationBudgetExhausted`, close sessions and retain cheap independently authorized monitoring. Never auto-renew a sensitive session into unbounded approval.

Ordinary sanitized network observation still consumes administrator CPU/rate/memory budgets. Short prepaid authority and freshness/revocation bounds apply to all sessions; long approval validity does not mean long disconnected observation. Sharing maxima, snapshot storage and action starts are independent limits. Pending/Spent observation counters and executionsReserved MUST be visible in the authoritative accounting model. A raw-filter predicate on otherwise sanitized output requires these same limits.

### SC-AUT-006

Given a 16 MiB raw ring with a 1 GiB observation grant, when more than 1 GiB would enter the sensitive pipeline, then observation stops even if no snapshot was frozen. Restarting the sensor or changing grantRef cannot reuse spent bytes. Logs and other independently admitted cheap context can remain armed.

## Composition and syscall authorization

CaptureProfile publication grants no execution authority. All contributing owner/source checks remain current. Friendly sharing selections resolve through the deduplication alias map to actual action/projection IDs before acceptance; several aliases never multiply a recipient grant or artifact count.

For syscall arguments, require the cumulative observed-event and pre-read byte limits in [syscalls](syscalls.md) before arming; absent event counters/limits cannot authorize argument tracing. Event reservations/spend share the existing SQL/local prepaid accounting and never reset on a trace restart. Raw syscall_query and raw sharing require their independent existing approvals. Metadata-only projections have a fixed no-argument schema and inherit artifact privacy/expiry.

## Approval timing by execution mode

Under [action lifecycles](action-lifecycle.md), ongoing HTTP/raw packet and syscall-argument recording requires reviewed acquisition authority before policy arming, including every observation preceding the first trigger. A grant requested only when the incident fires cannot authorize prior sensitive recording. OnTrigger dump/command/profile approvals authorize their bounded dispatches; they need not hold independently authorized window preservation hostage. Previews and reviews show these two costs and activation times separately. Arming, observation bytes/events, source-session starts and incident saves keep distinct bounded counters; none are reset by trigger cooldown or ring rotation.

## Manual saves under reviewed policy authority

REQ-SAV-001–004 are a narrowly typed exception to requiring a detector occurrence for an existing policy snapshot. A policy must explicitly enable recordingSaves in its accepted/reviewed execution scope. Current caller workload-maintainer collection rights and current policy executor/source authority are both required. Applicable grants must cover the sensitive source and save/output budget. The policy executor remains unchanged; the initiating human/agent does not acquire that grant or any raw evidence access. No grant can authorize a full ad hoc recipe through this route.

Manual saves have their own capture identity/digest and retained source audience. No per-save approval is added when the reviewed capability and remaining budgets already cover it; a missing policy authorization blocks collection. New per-capture sharing may be requested by an eligible owner under the ordinary rules. Automatic policy SharingIntents expressly exclude recordingSave-origin captures; never publish them solely because they reference that policy. Preserve source audience history and do not use new creator status to grant access.

## Live-source authorization

Approved live projections use [REQ-LIV-001](live-watch.md#req-liv-001--eligible-sources-and-current-authority): current maintainer/collection eligibility AND ordinary evidence-read entitlement under retained source audience, valid client/evidence:read scope, local live-read allowance, current source executor/acquisition grant/lease and no deny. A raw acquisition policy grant does not become a viewer's raw-query grant. Artifact shares, manager assignments and creator identity confer no live source access. Recheck at view open, cursor/read/update and actual delivery; scrub stale displayed rows on known denial and bound disconnected copies by per-row expiry. There is no live sharing or raw-live approval purpose; saving followed by existing artifact grants/shares remains the deeper-analysis path.

Artifact raw grants also support `download_artifact` for exact existing digests under [REQ-DLD-002](artifact-downloads.md). Reuse an unexpired covering grant, reserve full native output sizes and charge retransmission/uncertainty. Ordinary approved artifacts require their normal reader/share entitlement and download client scope without new human approval. Collection and query-only grants do not silently grant full raw export.
